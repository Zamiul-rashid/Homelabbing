My homelab: two servers, one router

Roles and data flows as of October 2026. Machine names, addresses and versions are left out on purpose.

Two servers, split by job

  • • App server: every app, DNS, the reverse proxy
  • • Proxmox host: monitoring, AI assistant, portfolio, game hub
  • • Media lives on the Proxmox host and is shared over NFS

Backups and the drive window

  • • Every disk job starts between 03:00 and 03:45
  • • Configs, Gitea, Paperless and music go nightly to the backup disk
  • • All configs are also in git, scanned for secrets first

Known gaps

  • • No offsite copy yet, apart from configs in git
  • • Nextcloud data has a single copy
  • • Restores never tested end to end; no UPS yet
