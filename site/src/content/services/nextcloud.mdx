## What I Ended Up With
My own private cloud storage hub accessible across Windows, Mac, Linux, iOS, and Android. I could sync documents across computers in real-time, share password-protected download links with clients or friends, edit office files, manage calendars, and keep my sensitive files stored safely on `/data/nextcloud` under my physical control.

## Why I Chose Nextcloud

Google Drive, Microsoft OneDrive, and Dropbox scanned my documents for policy violations, charged tiered fees, and could suspend my account without warning. Nextcloud kept my files on my own hardware and matched every collaboration feature I needed.

## How I Set It Up

### 1. Launching Nextcloud & MariaDB
I navigated to `stacks/cloud-storage/` and started the containers:

```bash
cd stacks/cloud-storage
docker compose up -d
```

### 2. First-Time Wizard Configuration
I opened my browser to `https://YOUR_SERVER_IP:4443`. Because LinuxServer Nextcloud generated an automatic self-signed SSL certificate on initial startup, my browser showed a standard warning (`Your connection is not private`). I clicked **Advanced → Proceed to YOUR_IP (unsafe)** to reach the wizard:

1. **Create Admin Account:** I entered my desired username and a strong password.
2. **Database Configuration:** My compose stack connected Nextcloud to MariaDB automatically. If prompted for database connection parameters:
   - **Database user:** `nextcloud`
   - **Database password:** I entered my `${DB_PASSWORD}` value from `.env`.
   - **Database name:** `nextcloud`
   - **Database host:** `nextcloud-db`
3. I clicked **Install / Finish Setup** and waited (~1 minute) while Nextcloud initialized my personal cloud.
