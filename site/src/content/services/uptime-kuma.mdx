## What I Ended Up With
A dashboard with a green or red bar for every app I run, plus a message on my phone when one stops responding and another when it comes back. It answers "is it down, or is it just me?" in one glance.

## Why I Chose Uptime Kuma

I used to find out an app was broken when someone in the house told me. Uptime Kuma is one container, has a clear interface, and needed no config files at all; everything is set up in the browser.

## How I Set It Up

### 1. Starting It
I ran the official image with a data volume and opened the web interface to create my account.

### 2. Monitors
For each app I added an HTTP monitor pointing at its web interface. For apps without one, a TCP or Docker container check did the job.

### 3. Alerts
I connected a notification service, sent a test, and attached it to every monitor. Then I stopped one container on purpose to confirm the alert arrived.
