## What I Ended Up With
A fast, private music server that indexed my entire FLAC, MP3, and ALAC audio collection. I could stream lossless audio from any browser or use third-party Subsonic apps for offline downloads, smart playlists, and scrobbling—no subscription required.

## How I Set It Up

### 1. Launching the Container
Once I placed my audio files in `/data/media/music`, I ran `docker compose up -d` in `stacks/music-server/`:
```bash
cd stacks/music-server
docker compose up -d
```

### 2. Initial Scan & Login
I opened `http://YOUR_SERVER_IP:4533` and created my admin profile (`username` and `password`). Navidrome immediately began scanning `/data/media/music` and populated my album art in seconds.

### 3. Connecting My Mobile Apps
Because Navidrome speaks the industry-standard **Subsonic API**, I could pick any compatible app instead of being locked into one:

- **Android:** **Symfonium** is my pick (unmatched equalizer, offline caching, and widget support) or **Substreamer** (free and minimalist).
- **iOS:** I recommend **Amperfy** (free, designed to feel exactly like Apple Music) or **Substreamer**.

To connect: I opened the app, entered `http://YOUR_SERVER_IP:4533` (or my domain/Tailscale URL), and signed in with my Navidrome credentials.
